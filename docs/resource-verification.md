# Resource verification workflow

Each resource record should gain `sourceUrl`, `sourceType`, `lastVerifiedAt`, `verifiedBy`, and `verificationStatus`. Verify against the provider's official site or by direct contact. Record the date, phone, address, populations served, accessibility, hours, intake steps, restrictions, and whether bed availability is live or unknown.

Recheck crisis and shelter records every 90 days and other services every 180 days. Show the verification date publicly. Flag stale records in the interface, suppress closed resources, preserve an audit log, and provide a prominent correction form. Search snippets and page copy must never imply live bed availability unless a live authoritative feed supplies it.
